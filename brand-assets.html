Usage Guidance

How the system should stay consistent

This is a practical working guide for keeping the current MarketLearn look recognizable as more pages and tools are added.

Typography

Editorial + practical

Use Cormorant Garamond for high-level headings and Source Sans 3 for interface text, labels, and paragraphs.

Color Balance

Warm neutrals first

Lead with warm paper tones, reserve Strategic Blue for calls to action and icon strokes, and use Brass Gold as a secondary supporting accent.

Icon Style

Single-outline forms

Keep icons simple, large in their available space, and consistent in stroke behavior without extra inner decoration.
